# Hello 👋
Here are all my Data Science projects and articles, organized into groups that cover the main stages of a data project: descriptive, diagnostic, predictive, and prescriptive analysis. Each phase is essential for gaining deep insights and adding strategic value. I've also included some of my earliest projects, which reflect my journey and growth in the field.

- **Descriptive Analysis:** To identify phenomena and initial patterns in the data.
- **Diagnostic Analysis:** To investigate the underlying causes of identified phenomena.
- **Predictive Analysis:** To recognize patterns in the data and forecast possible future scenarios and clear segmentations.
- **Prescriptive Analysis:** To suggest actions and strategic decisions based on the insights obtained.
